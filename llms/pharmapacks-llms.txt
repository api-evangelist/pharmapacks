# Pharmapacks

> Pharmapacks was a New York e-commerce marketplace seller and third-party logistics
> operator (founded 2010, Long Island City) that became one of the largest independent
> sellers of health, beauty and personal-care goods on Amazon, eBay and Walmart
> Marketplace. It rebranded as Packable Holdings LLC in 2021, abandoned a SPAC merger
> with Highland Transcend Partners I Corp. in March 2022, filed for Chapter 11
> bankruptcy on 2022-08-28, and ceased operations. This file is generated by API
> Evangelist from a public-surface profile; it is not published by the company.

## Status

- Pharmapacks / Packable Holdings LLC: OUT OF BUSINESS. Chapter 11 filed 2022-08-28;
  substantially all assets sold in the wind-down.
- pharmapacks.com: registered (GoDaddy, created 2009-03-21, expires 2027-03-21) but
  serving a parked-domain lander. Every path returns HTTP 200 with the same parking
  shell; treat any 200 from this host as a catch-all, not as content.
- packable.com: LIVE. A successor brand operating as Packable, whose published
  leadership includes Pharmapacks/Packable co-founder Jonathan Webb and former
  Packable finance lead Chris Cherwa. It sells fulfillment and logistics, digital
  enablement and value-added services. It publishes no developer program.

fulfillment platform (reported as "Master Brain"). It was an internal system and was
never exposed as a public developer product.
